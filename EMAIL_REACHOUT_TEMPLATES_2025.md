### Template 5C: Weekly Productivity Tips (Engagement Email)

**Subject:** 3 Kanban tips for [their use case]

```
Hi [First Name],

Happy [Day of week]! Here are 3 quick Kanban tips for [freelancers/developers/students/etc.]:

**1. The "Waiting" Column**
Don't let blocked tasks clog your "In Progress" column.

Add a "Waiting on..." column for:
• Client feedback
• Code reviews
• External dependencies

Keeps your active work visible and unblocked tasks out of sight.

**2. The 2-Minute Rule**
If a task takes < 2 minutes, don't add it to your board.

Just do it immediately.

Your board is for tasks that require focus and tracking, not quick wins.

**3. Weekly Reset**
Every Friday (or Monday), do a 5-minute board audit:
• Archive completed cards
• Re-prioritize remaining tasks
• Add new tasks from your backlog

Keeps your board from becoming a graveyard of old tasks.

**Try one this week and let me know how it goes!**

Want more tips? Join our Discord community where [X] users share their workflows: [Link]

See you next week,
[Your Name]

P.S. - Using Flow Board? Share your setup in our Discord #show-your-board channel!

---

[Unsubscribe] | [Update preferences]
```

**When to Send:** Weekly, on Mondays or Fridays

**Content Rotation:**
- Week 1: Kanban column structure tips
- Week 2: Productivity principles (WIP limits, etc.)
- Week 3: Use case spotlight (how X uses Flow Board)
- Week 4: Behind the scenes (how we built Y feature)

### A/B Testing Ideas

**Subject Lines:**
- **Personal vs Generic:** "Quick question, [Name]" vs "Try Flow Board"
- **Question vs Statement:** "Still using Trello?" vs "A better alternative to Trello"
- **FOMO vs Value:** "You're missing this" vs "Here's what you can do"

**Email Length:**
- Short (50-100 words) vs Medium (150-200 words)
- Personal/casual vs Professional/formal
- Text-only vs Text + images/GIFs

**Call-to-Action:**
- "Try Flow Board" vs "See it in action" vs "Create your first board"
- Single CTA vs Multiple CTAs
- Link vs Button

## ✅ Email Best Practices Checklist

Before sending any email:

- [ ] **Personalized subject line** (includes their name or specific reference)
- [ ] **Value proposition in first sentence** (what's in it for them?)
- [ ] **Single, clear CTA** (don't ask for 3 different things)
- [ ] **Mobile-friendly** (short paragraphs, scannable)
- [ ] **Proofread** (zero typos - use Grammarly)
- [ ] **Unsubscribe link** (for bulk emails - legally required)
- [ ] **Tested on mobile device** (50%+ of emails opened on mobile)
- [ ] **Followed up** (if no response in 5-7 days)

## 💡 Final Tips

1. **Personalization > Volume:** 10 highly personalized emails beat 100 generic ones

2. **Follow Up:** 80% of sales require 5+ follow-ups. Most people stop at 1.

3. **Test Everything:** Subject lines, send times, CTAs - A/B test constantly

4. **Track Metrics:** Use UTM codes in links to see which emails drive signups

5. **Be Human:** People buy from people, not companies. Let your personality show.

6. **Provide Value First:** Every email should give something (insight, tool, tip) before asking

7. **Respect Inbox:** If someone doesn't respond after 2 follow-ups, move on

**Remember:** Email is a long game. Build relationships, not just link clicks.
