## 9. KEY METRICS TO TRACK

### SaaS Metrics Investors Care About:

1. **MRR/ARR** - Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue
2. **Growth Rate** - Month-over-month %
3. **CAC** - Customer Acquisition Cost
4. **LTV** - Lifetime Value
5. **LTV:CAC Ratio** - Target 3:1 or higher
6. **Churn Rate** - Monthly % (target <5%)
7. **NPS** - Net Promoter Score
8. **Time to Value** - How fast users get value
9. **Activation Rate** - % who complete core action
10. **Free-to-Paid Conversion** - % who upgrade

### AI-Specific Metrics:

1. **AI Prompt Generation Rate** - % of users who use AI feature
2. **Prompts per User** - Average usage
3. **AI Feature Retention** - Do they come back for AI?
4. **Prompt Success Rate** - User satisfaction with AI outputs
